# Готель «Клеопатра»

Live site: https://kleopatra.hotelup.work

## About
"Klepoatra" is a 4-star hotel-restaurant complex in the Old Town of Kamianets-Podilskyi, at vul. Tatarska, 21. It has 82 rooms across four categories (Standard, Lux, Business with panoramic views, and VIP with a kitchenette), and positions itself as an all-in-one destination for weekend trips, family stays, and business events without needing to leave the property.

## Amenities
- 82 rooms (Standard, Lux, Business, VIP categories)
- Restaurant serving Ukrainian and European cuisine (breakfasts, dinners, celebrations, live music)
- SPA
- Swimming pool
- Bowling
- Billiards
- Conference hall (for conferences, presentations, trainings, corporate meetings)
- Photo gallery
